Its four final-policy episodes completed **4,800 controls, 24,000 normal steps and five compiler steps**; [`root_run_01_readback_01.json`](../results/d1_rolling_residual_speed_20260924/evidence/drive/root_run_01_readback_01.json) passed. Thus execution is valid and candidate qualification is false. The new law has no same-law zero-residual comparator, so the drive improvement cannot be attributed to RL.
